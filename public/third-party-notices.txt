Third-party notices for Plumb (https://plumb.isocline.ai)

Body diagram (src/lib/plumb/body/paths-*.ts)

The front and back body outlines on the Progress page come from
MuscleMap (https://github.com/melihcolpan/MuscleMap) by Melih Colpan, used under the MIT
License reproduced below. MuscleMap ships its path data as Swift source.
scripts/convert-musclemap.py converts it to TypeScript. It drops the sub-groups Plumb doesn't
draw (upper/lower chest and abs, inner/outer quad, front/rear deltoid) and leaves the remaining
artwork unchanged.
